Form 3CD to Form No. 26: the clause-by-clause crosswalk

By Chandan Gupta, CA, CIA · Updated 2026-11-14 · Free to use (CC BY 4.0)

From tax year 2026-27, the tax audit report moves from Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD to Form No. 26. This page maps every 3CD clause to its Form 26 counterpart: what is renumbered, changed, merged, split, new or dropped, and the data each clause will need. Use it to plan next season's working papers and client data requests.

Working draft. Built from the Gazette text of Form No. 26 (G.S.R. 198(E), 20 March 2026) and the CBDT FAQs, and not yet reviewed by a second practitioner. Rows marked Medium or Low confidence need a closer look. Always check the notified form, any corrigendum and the e-filing utility before you rely on a row. This is not professional advice.

What Form No. 26 is

Form No. 26 is the tax audit report and statement of particulars under section 63 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, prescribed by rule 47 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026. It was notified in the Gazette of India on 20 March 2026 (G.S.R. 198(E)). It replaces Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD (section 44AB and rule 6G of the 1961 law).

  • First year: it applies to tax years beginning on or after 1 April 2026, so the first Form 26 audit is for tax year 2026-27 (FAQ26 Q2).
  • Old forms continue for the last 1961-Act year: "Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD continue for tax audits for previous years relevant to assessment years up to 2026-27. However, from tax year 2026-27, tax audit has to be furnished in Form No. 26." (FAQ26 Q6). The audit of FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) therefore still uses 3CA/3CB/3CD.
  • Due date: one month before the return due date under section 263(1) (FAQ26 Q5). The e-filing portal FAQ gives 30 September 2027 for tax year 2026-27 (EF-FORMS Q30), unless CBDT extends it.
  • Section references: every section in Form 26 is a 2025 Act or 2026 Rules reference (FAQ26 Q7).

Layout (as printed in the Gazette)

Part Content Old form
A Audit report where accounts are audited under any other law 3CA
B Audit report where accounts are not audited under any other law 3CB
C Particulars of the assessee, items 1-8 3CD clauses 1-7
D Statement of particulars, clauses 9-53, in sections A to K, with schedules 3CD clauses 8-44

The CBDT FAQs (and the draft form) use different letters: A = particulars, B = statement, C = 3CA report, D = 3CB report. This crosswalk uses the Gazette letters.

Every Part D clause needs a Yes/No. A "Yes" is detailed in a schedule at the end of the form (FAQ26 Q15, Q17).

Biggest changes

New in Form 26 (no 3CD clause)

  • 14(b)-(e): accounting software; cloud or other storage with IP address and country; compliance with rule 46(8); address of the backup server in India.
  • 39: year-wise MAT/AMT credit and its use.
  • 42: head-office expenditure disallowable under section 60 (non-residents).
  • 43: remittances reported in Part D of Form 145 (ex-15CA).
  • 44: amounts not included as income under section 92(2)(m), with reasons.
  • 47: every acquisition or transfer of unquoted shares, with book value, FMV and the 92(2)(m) / section 79 amounts.
  • 48: loans or advances paid that are deemed dividend under section 2(40)(e).
  • 49: list of all TANs. 51(b) and 51(d): earlier-year TDS disallowances allowed this year. 33(c): earlier MSE (37(2)(g)) disallowances allowed this year.
  • Part C items 5-7: residential status, email, phone.
  • Parts A/B: a table of the income or book-profit impact of qualifications, and each qualification tied to a clause and typed as test check, management representation, unable to verify, or others.

Merged or restructured

  • 3CD 6 and 7 (previous year, assessment year) become one "tax year".
  • 3CD 13(b)+(c) become 15(b); 13(d)+(e) become 17.
  • 3CD 31 (269SS, 269ST, 269T) becomes clause 45, with mode codes A-L (cash, journal entries, transfers, conversions). 45(d) asks only for repayments not made by the permitted modes. 3CD listed every repayment over the limit.
  • 3CD 21 is spread across clauses 26, 27(a)-(d), 28, 30, 31 and 51, keyed to 2025 Act sections 14, 29, 31, 32, 34, 35 and 36.
  • 3CD 26 (section 43B) becomes clause 32: only the disallowed amounts (32(a)) and earlier disallowances allowed this year (32(b)). Amounts paid in time are no longer listed.
  • 3CD 20(b) (employee PF/ESI) becomes 27(b): only the disallowable amount by fund. The month-wise due-date table is gone.
  • 3CD 34(b) becomes 50(b): instead of "were all transactions reported?", the auditor gives counts of transactions reported and not reported, and the amount not reported, after the latest correction statement (FAQ26 Q38).
  • 3CD 18 becomes 36: additions split into 180 days or more / less than 180 days without dates (FAQ26 Q26).
  • 3CD 44 becomes 52(b): three lines (registered, unregistered, exempt) with no reconciliation (FAQ26 Q20).
  • 3CD 35 becomes 53: value columns are added next to quantities.

Dropped (no Form 26 counterpart)

3CD 11(c) books examined; 21(b)(iii) equalisation levy; 21(b)(iv) fringe benefit tax; 21(b)(v) wealth tax; 26(ii) indirect taxes passed through P&L; 27(a) CENVAT; 29 share premium over FMV (56(2)(viib)); 30C GAAR; 31(d)-(e) repayments received over the 269T limit (Low confidence); 36 dividend distribution tax; 38 central excise audit; 39 service tax audit; 41 demands and refunds under other laws; 43 country-by-country report.

Two things in the Gazette text to watch. (1) Clauses 20 and 21 look swapped: the clause 20 question is about section 92(2)(m) income, but the schedule headed "20" is the general list of amounts not credited to profit and loss, and the other way round for 21. This crosswalk follows the question text; rows that depend on it are marked Medium. (2) Part letters: the Gazette prints the 3CA-type report as Part A, the 3CB-type report as Part B, particulars as Part C and the statement as Part D, while the CBDT FAQs use the draft letters. Clause numbers are not affected.
