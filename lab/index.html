The five habits behind the desk

  • Brief him like a junior.What to do, which file and where, the rule, what comes back, and how you will check it.
  • Headings, not data.Never paste client or company data. Give the column headings; the AI writes a script you run on your own computer.
  • Jitna andar, utna bahar.Rows in equal rows out, and the totals tie to the source, before you trust a result.
  • Copies, never originals.Take a backup, work in a new folder of copies, and approve an agent’s plan before it touches anything.
  • The sign-off stays yours.The script finds exceptions. You decide what they mean.

How this works

These pages are plain HTML and a little JavaScript. There is no AI behind them, no sign-in, no tracking and no analytics, and the page is set to block any connection after it loads. What you type stays in this tab and is gone when you close it. The scores come from simple, visible rules, so treat them as practice, not a verdict on your work.
