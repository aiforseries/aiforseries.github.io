      Why each guardrail
      

      In Episode 8, Saaf kar do, Kapoor tells Gyaani to delete whatever is extra. To Gyaani, the cabinet was extra. So was the chair. The fix is one line from Madam: never the originals; take a backup, and run it only on copies.
      

        

      • Copies and a backupThe agent works in a folder that holds copies, and the originals are backed up somewhere it cannot reach.
        • 

      • Plan first, then your OKYou see what it means by “extra” or “duplicate” before it acts on a single file.
        • 

      • New files onlyNothing is deleted, moved or overwritten. Results go into an Output folder, with a log of every change.
        • 

      • Counts and totalsFiles in equal files out; rows and amounts tie for each file. Stop on errors instead of guessing.
        • 

      • Deletions are your callIt may propose a list. It deletes nothing. The sign-off stays with you.
