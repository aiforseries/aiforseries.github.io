    The habit, in three steps

    1. Look before you pasteAnything in a chat box goes to the AI company’s servers. Ledgers, statements and emails are full of PANs, account numbers and names.
    2. Give only the headingsDate, Party Name, PAN, Amount. That is all the AI needs to write a script for your file. Not one data row.
    3. Run it on your computerThe script reads the real file on your laptop and prints only counts and totals. Check rows in equal rows out, and that the totals agree.

    What it looks for

    • PAN: five letters, four digits, one letter (AAAAA9999A)
    • GSTIN: the 15-character pattern (state code, PAN, entity, Z, check)
    • Aadhaar-like numbers: 12 digits in groups of four, or 12 digits that pass the Aadhaar check digit
    • IFSC: four letters, a zero, six letters or digits
    • Account-like numbers: runs of 9 to 18 digits, and any long number in an Account column
    • Emails and phone numbers: Indian mobiles (with or without +91) and landlines
    • Names: whole cells in Name, Party, Vendor, Customer, Payee or Employee columns, and business or person names inside Narration and Particulars
    • Amounts and dates: 1,23,456.00, ₹ and Rs. amounts, Dr/Cr, and the common date formats

    This is pattern matching in your browser, not a guarantee. It can miss things and flag harmless ones; you stay the reviewer. The page makes no network requests once loaded: its security policy blocks them, and there is nothing to send them to.
