    Why counts and totals?

    • 0Zero says nothing about missing rowsA difference of zero only means the rows that made it in agree with each other. It is silent about the rows that never made it in.
    • =Counts catch drops, totals catch changesRows in equal rows out shows nothing was dropped or doubled. Totals that agree show nothing changed on the way.
    • ΣCount only real entriesSubtotals, blank lines and repeated headings inflate a count. Leave them out, then review every exception yourself.

    Ask the AI for a script that prints these

    Give it only the column headings (the prompt fills them in from your files once you drop them). The script runs on your computer and prints the control totals, not your data.

    

    Bhasad & Associates · Episode 4Difference zero: match the count and the totals firstKapoor is thrilled with a two-second bank reco. 3,000 entries went in; 300 came out. The skit and its prompt.
    

    What it reads
    

    • CSV or TSV, with commas or tabs, quoted fields, a byte-order mark, and title lines above the headings.
      • 

    • Indian amounts like 1,23,456.00, ₹ and Rs., brackets or a minus for negatives, Dr and Cr after the amount or in their own column, and separate Withdrawal and Deposit columns.
      • 

    • Rows it leaves out of the count: blank rows, repeated heading rows, and total, subtotal, page total, opening and closing balance rows. Each one is listed so you can check.
      • 

    • Matching: by a key column such as a reference number when both files have one, otherwise by date and amount.
    

    Totals are added in paise, so there is no rounding drift. This checks completeness, not correctness: a matching count and total do not prove each match is right.
