      How the score works
      

      No AI reads your brief. A few plain rules look for words a junior would need, so a good brief in other words may score lower. Use it as a prompt to think, not a mark.
      

        

      • 1. The taskA job word (reconcile, match, list, flag, scrutinise…) and what it is done on.
        • 

      • 2. The data, and where it isFile or folder names, and “only the headings”. Anything that looks like a PAN, GSTIN, account number, email or pasted table fails this check.
        • 

      • 3. The condition or ruleOne of: a threshold or limit, a match key, “within 3 days”, “only…”, what to ignore, or your own master file. A period on its own counts as half.
        • 

      • 4. The outputWhat to save or give back (a workbook, a table) and its sheets or columns.
        • 

      • 5. The checksTwo of: counts, totals, “tie” or “agree”, rows that add back, “verify against”, unverified items.
        • 

      • 6. Safety and judgementIt runs on your computer or on copies, and you keep the decision: “list them; I will decide”.
        •
