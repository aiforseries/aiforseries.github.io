“AI kaunsa Act padh raha hai?”

The mistake

Asking the AI for a section without the tax year. It answers from whichever Act it learnt.

The habit

Check the tax year first, then the Act, then the section.

The free prompt

Tax year first, then the Act, then the section

When to use: Before you ask an AI for any section number, especially when a law has been replaced and the old one still governs earlier years.

My question is about Indian income tax for this tax year or period: <tax year or period>.
Before you answer:
1. Tell me which Act governs that tax year, and why. Do not assume the newest Act applies: an earlier Act still governs earlier years. If you are not sure which Act applies to this year, say so and stop.
2. Say whether you are reading the law from a document I gave you or recalling it from memory. Mark anything from memory UNVERIFIED.
Then answer:
3. The section of that Act that applies, marked FROM MEMORY if recalled, and what it covers, in plain words.
4. If I also need the matching section in the other Act, give it only by quoting the row from the official mapping table I have given you. If I have not given you one, write "Check the official mapping" and do not guess.
My question: <your question>

A new prompt for this episode, in the style of the free prompt library. The AI For Series, Chandan Gupta. Licence: CC BY 4.0. Replace the parts in <angle brackets>.

→What to give the AI

Your question and the tax year or period it is about. If you have it, the official mapping table between the old and new Act, as a file. No client facts are needed.

Never paste client or company data into a chat. Give the headings and the structure; the work runs on your own computer.

←What you get back

Which Act governs that tax year and why, the section that applies (marked FROM MEMORY if recalled) in plain words, and a matching section in the other Act only if quoted from the official mapping you gave it.

Check before you rely on it

  • Check the tax year yourself, then confirm which Act governs it from the official source.
  • Read the section's current text in that Act. Where you need the other Act, find the row in the official mapping yourself: renumbered does not mean unchanged.

Every time: the control totals agree, rows in equal rows out, the law is checked in the official source, and the judgement stays with you.
