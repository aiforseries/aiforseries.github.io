Bhasad & Associates

A fictional CA firm and its client company.

A claymation comedy about using AI at work, for everyone in finance: CA firms, company finance teams, internal auditors, tax practitioners and CA students. Across the corridor from the firm is its client, Kapoor Industries. A small robot, Gyaani, works for both offices, and both use him wrong. Each episode ends with the right way and one free prompt. Pick an episode, copy the prompt.

  • 1Section 420½ Firm“AI ka source kya hai?”Ask for every section with its source, then check the source yourself.
  • 2Difference zero Company“Reco ho gaya? Gino.”Match the count and the totals first: as much in as out, before you trust the difference.
  • 3Poora ledger Company“Company ka data chat mein?”Only the column headings go to the AI; run the script on your own laptop.
  • 4Audit kar do Firm“AI ko aadha kaam bataya?”Give a scoped brief: match the big bank payments to the voucher register; the unmatched ones go into Excel.
  • 5Saaf kar do Company“AI agent ko original diye?”Never the originals: take a backup, then work only on copies.
  • 6Chat se hisaab Company“AI se total poocha?”Get the formula from the AI; get the total from Excel.
  • 7AI ne bola Firm“AI ne aapke facts dekhe?”The AI's answer isn't final: check the source and the facts, both.
  • 8Shortcut Firm“AI se answers likhwaye?”Ask the AI to quiz you and catch your mistakes, not to solve for you.
  • 9Sab fraud hai Company“Har exception fraud hai?”The script finds exceptions; we make the findings.
  • 10Ek client, sau client Firm“Ek ka kaam, sau pe?”Test on one, run the batch, check rows and totals for every client, review the exceptions. The sign-off stays yours.
  • 11Purana Act, naya zamana Firm“AI kaunsa Act padh raha hai?”Check the tax year first, then the Act, then the section.

The method behind every prompt: never paste client or company data into a chat; give the AI the headings and let a script run on your own computer; check the control totals and that rows in equal rows out; verify the law in the official source; keep the judgement with you.
