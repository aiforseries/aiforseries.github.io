    Questions

    Which book should I start with?

    If you are new to AI tools, start with the free Which AI, Which Plan?. Then pick the book for your work above: CA studies and articleship, CA practice, tax practice, internal audit, or Excel and finance.

    Where can I buy the books?

    All six are on Google Play Books. The five paid books are also on Kindle, on Amazon.in and Amazon.com. Prices are on each store's page.

    Do I need to know how to code?

    No. The AI writes and runs the scripts; the books show you how to ask, check the result and keep data on your own computer.

    Which AI tool do the books use?

    The profession books use Claude Code and, in the Excel book, Claude for Excel. Claude Code needs a paid Claude plan. The free starter book covers ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot.

    Is this an exam shortcut?

    No. In AI for CA Students every prompt keeps you doing the thinking: the AI checks your working, quizzes you and explains, rather than handing over answers.
