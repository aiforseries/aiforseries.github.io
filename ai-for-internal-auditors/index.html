Who it’s for

Internal auditors, SOX and ICFR testers, audit managers and CAEs. The whole internal audit lifecycle with Claude Code: risk assessment, planning, walkthroughs, control testing and sampling, full-population analytics, fraud red flags, working papers, observations, audit committee reporting and follow-up. Instead of pasting an AP extract into a chat window, the AI writes a small script that runs on your laptop against the full population, so only column headers and results leave your machine, and a reviewer can re-perform the test.

What it isn’t: Not for pasting company data into a chat window: the analysis runs on your laptop, and only column headers and results leave it.

Questions

Who is this book for?

Internal auditors, SOX and ICFR testers, audit managers and chief audit executives who want to use AI on real engagements, not just for drafting emails.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Claude Code writes and runs the scripts; the book shows you how to ask for them, read the result and check it against control totals.

What about company data and confidentiality?

Chapter 1 explains what an AI tool actually sends and what stays on your laptop. The method keeps the data local: the AI sees column headers and results, and the analysis runs on your machine.

What do I need to follow along?

A Windows or Mac laptop and a paid Claude plan, since Claude Code is not in the free plan. The book links to its practice files and answer keys.

Is it affiliated with Anthropic or The IIA?

No. It is an independent book, not endorsed by either.

Where can I buy it, and in what format?

As an ebook on Google Play Books and on Kindle (Amazon.in and Amazon.com). Google Play works on a phone, tablet and the web; Kindle on any Kindle app or device. Prices are on each store's page.
